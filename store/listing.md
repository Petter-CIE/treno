Varde Fitness is a training partner that does everything in one place – and asks for nothing in return. No subscription, no ads and no tracking. No account needed – your data stays on your phone, with an optional email backup if you want it.

ONE PLAN FOR EVERYTHING YOU TRAIN
Gym on Monday, a run on Wednesday, kettlebells at home on Friday and ten minutes of mobility in the morning – all in one weekly plan and one log.

READY-MADE PROGRAMS
• Gym for beginners and Push / Pull / Legs
• Home workouts with no equipment
• Your first pull-up and calisthenics strength
• Zero to 5K – run/walk intervals with a voice coach
• Kettlebell start and Swing + Get-up
• HIIT conditioning and morning mobility

A TIMER THAT COACHES YOU
Sets with rest, EMOM, AMRAP, Tabata, intervals and run/walk – with a voice coach that announces every exercise and counts down, vibration, and a screen that stays on.

70 ANIMATED EXERCISES
Every exercise has its own animation and short technique cues. Filter by discipline and see only what fits the equipment you own – even your exact kettlebell sizes.

TRACK YOUR PROGRESS
Log weights, distance and pace, rate how hard it felt, and follow your progress in charts. Hit your weekly goal and build a streak.

12 LANGUAGES
English, Norwegian, Czech, German, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Italian, Polish, Swedish, Danish and Finnish – including the voice coach.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account is needed and there is no tracking. Everything is stored on your device. If you want, sign in with your email to back up your plan and log and get them back on a new phone – you can delete the account and backup at any time in the app.

### Apple – App Privacy („nutriční štítek“)
Kvůli nepovinnému účtu se zálohou se **nedá** zvolit „Data Not Collected“. Vyplň:
- **Contact Info → Email Address** – Linked to user: **Yes** · Tracking: **No** · Purpose: **App Functionality**
- **Health & Fitness → Fitness** (tréninkový deník) – Linked: **Yes** · Tracking: **No** · Purpose: **App Functionality**
- **User Content → Other User Content** (plán, nastavení) – Linked: **Yes** · Tracking: **No** · Purpose: **App Functionality**
- Vše ostatní: Not collected. Tracking celkově: **No**.
- Smazání účtu přímo v appce je hotové (Nastavení → Účet a záloha → Smazat účet) – Apple to od appek s účtem vyžaduje (5.1.1(v)).
- Přihlášení je jen e-mailem (bez Google/Facebook), proto není potřeba „Sign in with Apple“.

### Google Play – Data safety
- Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? **Yes** (jen u nepovinného účtu)
- Is all user data encrypted in transit? **Yes**
- Account creation: **Yes – username/email, optional** („users can use the app without an account“)
- Delete account URL: `https://cieas.no/varde/#delete-account`
- Collected (not shared): **Personal info → Email address** a **Name** (jen při přihlášení přes Google); **Health and fitness → Fitness info**; **App activity → Other user-generated content** (plán, nastavení)
  - u všech: Collected **Yes**, Shared **No**, Processed ephemerally **No**, Required or optional: **Optional**, Purpose: **App functionality**, (u e-mailu navíc **Account management**)
- Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted? **Yes** – v appce i přes odkaz výše.

### Apple – App Review poznámka (Review Notes)
> The app works fully offline and needs no account. All data is stored locally on the device. Optionally, users can sign in with their email (one-time code, no password) in Settings → Account & backup to back up their plan and log; the account and backup can be deleted in the app (Settings → Account & backup → Delete account). Signing in is never required to use any feature. The "Support" button in Settings opens our website with an optional voluntary gift via PayPal; it does not unlock any content or features – the app is identical for everyone (Guideline 3.2.1(vii)).
